Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Every paid option is a one-time payment and nothing renews.

What is the difference between the Scan and the Fix it pack?

The Scan is the full report once. The Fix it pack is the same report plus 30 days of one-click re-checks and score history, so you can fix issues and watch the score change.

Do I need to prove I own the site?

Yes, for paid reports. After you pay, you add a DNS TXT record or a meta tag, then the scan runs. The free check does not need it.

Can I get a refund?

Ask within 7 days of paying by emailing support@deployvet.com. Refunds are not automatic. See the refund policy.

Does a good score mean my site is secure?

No. It means we did not find the issues we check for. DeployVet is not a penetration test, audit or certification. See the methodology.
