What we roast

Everything a stranger could find. Sorry in advance.

We look at your live site from the outside, the way a stranger would. Each thing we find gets a burn. Then a real fix, because we're not monsters.

  • Exposed keys

    not the service_role key in the frontend. anyone can copy it, and the bill is yours.

    We search your public JS for keys that belong on a server: Stripe, OpenAI, Supabase service_role and more.

  • Open database

    RLS is off. "the AI handled auth." the AI handled nothing.

    We use your public key to test if Row Level Security keeps strangers out.

  • Exposed files

    your-app.com/.env returning 200 is crazy. you didn't get hacked bestie, you hosted an open house.

    We look for env files, git folders, backups and admin or API routes with no login.

  • Headers

    no CSP, no HSTS, no nothing. "claude said it was secure."

    We check CSP, HSTS, framing and the other headers that block common attacks.

  • HTTPS

    HTTPS page, http files. wearing the seatbelt as a scarf.

    We flag missing HTTPS, missing redirects and files loaded over http.

  • Broken pages

    "it works on my preview." production would like a word.

    We find broken pages and links before your users do. Plus basic SEO misses.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Still stuck? Email support@deployvet.com.

What is DeployVet?

DeployVet checks apps built with AI and no-code tools for security holes and bugs, then roasts them a little. It was made by Hayden Young. It used to be called Launch Guard.

Will the roast be mean to me?

No. We roast the app, never the person. Pick Mild if you want it gentle. Nuclear is louder, but still about the code.

Which tools do you support?

Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Cursor, v0 and Bubble. Apps that use Supabase work too.

Is this a penetration test?

No. DeployVet is not a penetration test. It finds common, known issues. If your app holds very private data or payments, get a pro security review too.

Do you need my code or database?

Not for the free roast. It only looks at your public site. The paid Scan asks for read-only access to your repo or Supabase project. We can read but not change anything.

Can I check an app I don't own?

No. For paid scans and the scoreboard, you must prove you own the site with a DNS TXT record or a meta tag.

Will you charge me later?

No surprise charges. You pay once, up front, for a Scan ($7) or the Fix it pack ($10). Neither one is a subscription, so nothing renews.

Does a clean report mean my app is safe?

No tool can promise that. A clean report means we did not find the issues we check for.

Does DeployVet fix my app?

No. We never change your app. We tell you what is wrong and give you steps and prompts to fix it.
