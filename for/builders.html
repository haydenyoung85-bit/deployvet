How it works

Roast, fix, check again.

  1. 1

    Roast it free

    Your score, grade and your worst issue with its fix. We look at your live site from the outside, like a stranger would.

  2. 2

    Fix it for $10

    The Fix it pack lists every issue with copyable fix prompts and a PDF. One payment, no subscription, nothing renews.

  3. 3

    Check again

    The pack includes 30 days of "Did I fix it?" re-checks, so you can watch the score move.

After you pay for a paid scan, you prove you own the site with a DNS record or a meta tag. Then the scan runs. DeployVet is not a penetration test, and a clean report is not a promise that your app is safe.

Launching an app too? The Launch Pack ($39 once) is AI-written launch copy, delivered in 48 hours. Marketing, not security.

Questions

Is the $10 a subscription?

No. You pay once. Nothing renews.

Do you need my code?

Not for the free roast. It only looks at your public site. The paid Scan can also do a read-only check of a public repo or Supabase project if you choose to share one.

Does DeployVet fix my app?

No. We never change your app. We tell you what is wrong and give you steps and prompts to fix it.
