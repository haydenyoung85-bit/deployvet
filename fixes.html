Flat-price fixes

Fixes for the problems a scan finds

I'm Hayden Young. I run DeployVet from Oklahoma. If a scan finds problems on your site or app, I can fix them for a flat price that you agree to in writing before I start.

Who I am

I'm one person, not a company with a team. Claude, an AI assistant, helps me with the code, and every fix is checked with a DeployVet rescan before I send it to you. DeployVet itself is the same check anyone can run for free on the homepage.

The free first step

Run the free check on your site, or ask me and I'll send you the result. You get the score and the most important issue with how to fix it. You don't have to buy anything.

Prices

  • Quick fix, $150. The top 3 issues from your scan, fixed.
  • Standard, $300. The top 8 issues fixed, plus a retest.
  • Full cleanup, from $400. I look at the project first, then send you a quote.

How it works

  1. You get your scan results and the list of top issues.
  2. I send a short list of fixes and a flat price. Nothing starts until you agree.
  3. I make the fixes through your repository or a project invite.
  4. I rescan and send a short report on what changed and anything still open.

What is not included

  • This is not a penetration test or a full security audit.
  • There are no guarantees that a site is fully secure or will never go down. The work covers only the issues we agree on.
  • I only work on sites and apps you own or are allowed to change.
  • I never ask for your passwords. Access is through your own repository or a project invite.
  • Start dates depend on my current workload.

Get in touch

Email support@deployvet.com with your site address and what you're seeing. I answer by email, and if you'd rather not hear from me again, say so and I won't write again.
