These terms are the rules for using DeployVet at deployvet.com. DeployVet is run by Hayden Young. By using the site or buying a scan, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use DeployVet.

1. What DeployVet does

DeployVet checks websites and web apps from the outside for common, externally visible security problems and bugs. It gives you a report and steps to fix what it finds. DeployVet is not a penetration test, an audit or a certification. A clean report means we didn't find the issues we check for. It does not mean your site is safe. See the methodology.

2. Only check sites you own

You may only scan a site you own or have written permission to test. For paid scans you must prove you own the site with a DNS TXT record or a meta tag. Don't use DeployVet to attack, overload or snoop on anyone else's site.

3. How the checks work

  • The Free Check looks only at your public site, like a visitor would.
  • The paid scan can also read a public GitHub repo or Supabase project you give us. We only read. We never change your app, code or data.
  • If we find data that anyone can read, we note the table and column names. We don't keep the rows.
  • Never give us a secret key, password or service_role key. We only need public info.

  • DeployVet Free Check: $0.
  • DeployVet Scan: $7, one time. You pay first, then the scan runs.
  • DeployVet Fix it pack: $10, one time, for one site. Includes the full report, fix prompts, PDF, and 30 days of re-checks and score history. It does not renew.
  • DeployVet Pro (no longer sold): existing subscriptions keep working, renew until you cancel, and can be canceled any time from the billing portal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Prices are in US dollars. Stripe handles all payments, and we never see your full card number. We'll tell you before we change a price. A change never affects a period you already paid for.
