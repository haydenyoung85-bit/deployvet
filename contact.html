Contact

Contact DeployVet

Email is the way to reach us. We reply by email.

Email: support@deployvet.com

DeployVet is run by Hayden Young. We do not have a phone line or a public office address.

What to include

  • The address of the site you are asking about.
  • The email you used if you paid for something.
  • What happened and what you expected, in your own words.

Common reasons to write

  • Order or report problems. A scan that failed, a missing report email, or a charge you do not recognise.
  • Refunds. Ask within 7 days of paying. See the refund policy.
  • A finding that looks wrong. Send the address and the finding. See the methodology for what we check.
  • Your data. To see, correct or delete data, see the privacy policy and email the same address.
  • A security problem with deployvet.com itself. Please email us with the details before sharing them publicly.
  • Agencies and freelancers. Questions about checking several client sites or the Launch Pack.

Please do not send passwords, secret keys or card numbers by email.

Outside-in website security and bug checks. DeployVet is not a penetration test, an audit or a certification, and a clean result is not a guarantee.
