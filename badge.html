Badge

Survived the Roast

Get an A on a DeployVet roast and you can put this badge on your site. It links back to DeployVet. That's it.

How to get it

  1. Run a free roast on your own site.
  2. Score 90 or more (grade A).
  3. Copy the badge code from your results and paste it into your site.

The code

What it means (and doesn't)

The badge means your site passed our outside check on the day you ran it. It is not a promise that your app is safe, and it is not a penetration test. If your score drops later, please take the badge down until you fix things.
