Badge

Get your badge

If your latest DeployVet check is an A or a B, you can show it on your own site. The badge is drawn by DeployVet from the stored scan, so it always shows the real grade, score and date.

Who can have oneSites whose latest check is an A (90 to 100) or a B (75 to 89). There is no badge for a lower grade or for a site we have not checked.
What it showsThe grade, score and date of our stored check, for example “A 92/100 Oct 26 2026”. It links to the public result page.
Old resultsAfter 30 days the badge turns grey and says it is old. If a newer check scores below B, the badge stops showing. Re-check to refresh it.
What it is notAn outside-in check at a point in time. It is not a certification, an audit or a guarantee that a site is secure. Please do not describe it as one.
Use it on your own sitesOnly place the badge on sites you own or manage. The result is public for a domain, so to have a result removed, email support@deployvet.com.

How the grades work is on How we grade. No check yet? Run the free check.
