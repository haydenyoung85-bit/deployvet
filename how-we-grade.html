How we grade

A DeployVet check looks at what a stranger can reach on your public site. It gives a score out of 100 and a grade. Here is what the grades and the badge mean.

GradeScoreIn plain words
A90 to 100No serious issues found in what we check.
B75 to 89Mostly in good shape, with some smaller issues.
C60 to 74Several issues worth fixing.
D40 to 59Significant issues.
FBelow 40Serious issues, such as exposed secrets.

How the score is worked out

Every check starts at 100. Each finding takes off points by severity, and a critical or high security finding caps the score. The full table is on the methodology page.

What the badge shows

  • The grade, score and date of the latest check we have stored for that domain.
  • Only A and B results get a badge. It is drawn by us from our own record, so it cannot be edited to show a better grade.
  • It turns grey and says it is old after 30 days, and it stops showing if a newer check is below B.
  • It links to the public result page for the domain.

What it does not mean

  • It is an outside-in check at one moment. We do not log in, test your code or review how your business handles data.
  • It is not a certification, an audit, a penetration test or a guarantee. A good grade means we did not find the issues we check for.
  • We can miss things. Findings and scores come from fixed rules, not from a person reviewing your site.

See what we check and what we do not, or get your badge if you have an A or B.
