How it works

How a check works

Enter an address, read the result, and decide whether you want the full report. For paid reports you pay first, and there are no subscriptions.

1. Run the free check

Enter the address of a site you own or manage for a client. No signup and no card. In under a minute you get a score out of 100, a grade from A to F, and the most important finding with its severity and a fix. The other findings are counted but not shown.

2. Read the result

Each finding has a severity (critical, high, medium, low or info), a plain description, the address where it was seen and what to change. Findings that may be intentional, such as a table that looks like a public product list, are marked as such. A score of 90 or more is an A. How scores work.

3. Get the full report (optional)

If there is more than one finding, you can buy the Scan ($7) or the Fix it pack ($10). Both are one-time payments made on Stripe. You pay before the scan runs, and we never see your card details.

  • Confirm you own the site. After payment you add a DNS TXT record or a meta tag so we know the scan is authorised. Sites you do not control cannot be scanned in depth.
  • Optional extras. You may give us a public GitHub repository or a Supabase project address. We only read public information and never change anything. Never send a secret key.
  • The report. Every finding with evidence and a fix, fix prompts you can paste into your builder, and a PDF. It is emailed to you.

4. Fix and check again

Work through the findings, then run the check again to see the score change. The Fix it pack includes 30 days of one-click re-checks and score history. DeployVet does not change your site. You or your developer make the fixes.

For agencies and freelancers

Run the free check on a client site before handoff and keep the result. If you also want launch copy for the site, the Launch Pack ($39) is a separate, written-with-AI marketing product, not a security service.

Good to know

  • Only check sites you own or have the owner's permission to check.
  • The check is outside-in and automated. It is not a penetration test. See the methodology.
  • Refunds are covered in the refund policy. Questions: support@deployvet.com.
