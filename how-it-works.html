How it works

How a check works

Paste your link. See what a stranger can already see. If you want it fixed, pay $60 once and we do it. Fixed within 48 hours of getting access, or your money back.

1. Run the free check

Paste the address of an app you made, including one on Lovable, Bolt, Vercel, Netlify, or Replit. No signup and no card. In about 10 seconds you get a score out of 100 and each problem we can name, in plain words.

2. Read the result

You see the score and each problem's name and how bad it is: critical, high, medium, low, or info. The words say what could happen to you. The exact change for a high or critical problem stays with the paid fix. A score of 90 or more is an A. How scores work.

3. We fix it (optional)

The main offer is $60, once. We fix the problems the check found. You pay on Stripe. We never see your card.

  • Then tell us how to get in. A short form asks for the site, what it is built with, the best email, and how to invite us (a GitHub repo invite or a collaborator invite). Notes are optional.
  • You do not add a DNS record first. The invite is how we know we may change the project. We do not start before that invite.
  • The promise. Fixed within 48 hours of getting access, or your money back.

If you only want the written list, the $7 scan is still there. That one is a report, not a fix. For that report you pay, prove you own the site unless we already verified it, then an automated check runs. We email a link to the report. You can print or save a PDF from that page.

4. We make the change

We edit the project you invited us to, then run the check again. We do not touch the app before you invite us. Other prices, including a monthly check, are on the pricing page.

For agencies and freelancers

The check is built for one person looking at their own app. If you also want launch copy, the Launch Pack is a separate writing product, not a security fix. See pricing.

Good to know

  • Only check sites you own or have the owner's permission to check.
  • The check is outside-in and automated. It is not a penetration test. See the methodology.
  • Refunds are covered in the refund policy. Questions: support@deployvet.com.
