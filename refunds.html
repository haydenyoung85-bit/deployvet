Refund Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version: If DeployVet didn't work for you, email us within 7 days of paying. Hayden Young, who owns DeployVet, reviews every refund request himself and replies by email.

How to ask

Email support@deployvet.com from the email you paid with. Tell us your app's address and what went wrong. You don't need special words.

What happens next

  • The owner reads every request. Refunds are never automatic, and no bot decides them.
  • We'll reply within [RESPONSE_TIME] with a yes, a no and the reason, or a question.
  • If it's approved, the money goes back to your card through Stripe. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

When we usually say yes

  • The scan failed or never finished, and we couldn't fix it.
  • You were charged twice, or charged by mistake.
  • You asked within 7 days of paying, and the report didn't help you.

DeployVet Pro

You can cancel Pro any time, and you won't be charged again. Canceling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you ask within 7 days of a new charge, the owner will review a refund for that charge.

Free Check

The Free Check costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund.

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under the law where you live.
