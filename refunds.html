Refund Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version: If DeployVet didn't work for you, email us within 7 days of paying. We reply by email.

How to ask

Email support@deployvet.com from the email you paid with. Tell us your site's address and what went wrong. You don't need special words.

What happens next

  • Refunds are not automatic. Each request is looked at individually.
  • We'll reply by email with a yes, a no and the reason, or a question.
  • If it's approved, the money goes back to your card through Stripe. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

When we usually say yes

  • The scan failed or never finished, and we couldn't fix it.
  • You were charged twice, or charged by mistake.
  • You asked within 7 days of paying, and the report didn't help you.

DeployVet Fix it pack

The $10 Fix it pack is a one-time payment and never renews. If the scan can't run, or you ask within 7 days, we will review a refund.

DeployVet Launch Pack

The $39 Launch Pack is a one-time payment and never renews. If we can't deliver it, or you ask within 7 days, we will review a refund. We can't promise traffic or sales, so a pack that was delivered but didn't bring either is not by itself a reason for a refund.

DeployVet Pro

Pro is $29/mo. You can cancel any time, and you won't be charged again. Canceling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you ask within 7 days of a new charge, we will review a refund for that charge.

Free Check

The Free Check costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund.

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under the law where you live.

Outside-in website security and bug checks. DeployVet is not a penetration test, an audit or a certification, and a clean result is not a guarantee.
